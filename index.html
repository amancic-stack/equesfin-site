Who we are

An independent advisory group, close to the ground.

Eques Fin LLC is an independent advisory group, active since 1997, with structures in the USA and the UAE and partner offices in Europe and Africa. Our partners have advised on numerous M&A, fundraising and LBO transactions, with particular expertise in Consulting, Tech & Telco, Fintech, Industries, Agri-food, Infrastructure, Green Energy, Health, Transport & Logistics, Hospitality and Real Estate.

We act for founders, shareholders, family businesses and investors — on sell-side and buy-side M&A mandates, fundraising and strategic projects. As investors, we also hold positions in European and African companies, with a particular focus on the UEMOA and CEMAC zones, and Guinea-Conakry.
