6. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you have the right to access, rectify and erase your data, to restrict or object to its processing, to data portability, and to withdraw your consent at any time without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing. If you are in the European Union, you can also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority (in France, the CNIL — www.cnil.fr).

7. Security

The website is served exclusively over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection, and access to the data we receive is limited to the people who need it.
